Your table, your move.

Invite a friend to an online table, play Stockfish at three training levels, or share this device. Online seats are fixed; refresh or reopen your invitation to reconnect. Moves are accepted only on your turn. Resignation and draw claims are also available on your turn. Choose your opponent and color in the game menu, then start a fresh game. White moves first. Tap a piece and a destination, or drag it there. Rings mark captures.

Castling: select the king and move it two squares. En passant and promotion appear automatically when legal. A red square marks a king in check.

Use the move journal or ‹ / › to review. Return to Live to continue playing. Review never changes your saved match.

F flips the board. Escape clears your selection and opens the game menu. Arrow keys navigate squares; Enter or Space selects one. F flips the board. Computer play pauses while the menu, a dialog or move review is open. The training levels are not rated Elo.
