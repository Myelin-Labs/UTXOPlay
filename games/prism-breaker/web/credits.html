Prism Breaker

Game, Myelin integration, rendering and interface by Myelin Labs / UTXOPlay.

Original artwork

The crystal bricks, paddle, orb, optical lens, fragments, impact sprite, lives, two environments and key art were created with OpenAI imagegen. Source images, prompts and the sprite extraction pipeline are included in the repository.

Open-source foundation

The Focus Lens uses a segment-interception helper adapted from Jake Gordon’s JavaScript Breakout, revision eed59e2affa9423b93d2ac8ff93061bb88b33284. It is distributed under the MIT license. The project’s collision, input and rendering implementations were also studied. Its artwork and audio are not included.

The lens shows a fading near-field direction cue and stops before the first bounce. It never marks a landing target. It does not move the paddle, grant extra points or change your saved input tape.

Myelin

Original integer game rules run and replay through Myelin v0.31. Signed host replay is off-chain; this game does not settle results on CKB.
