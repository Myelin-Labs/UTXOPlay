## Original visual assets

Produced for this project with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool on 2026-10-11. Original generated visual assets are distributed with this project under MIT; no third-party game images were supplied as inputs. Alpha is preserved for sprite sheets. The following briefs record the production direction.

### `web/assets/islands.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Panoramic playfield: original whimsical floating islands at golden morning, turquoise sky, peach clouds, layered teal mountains, moss, wildflowers and waterfalls into clouds. Hand-painted gouache game art with shaded volumes and warm directional light. Orthographic side view; center 80% mostly clear sky. Continuous moss-covered cliff top across the bottom 15%, terracotta strata below. No characters, towers, slingshot, text or logos.

### `web/assets/characters.png`

Transparent 4-column × 2-row original game sprite sheet with separated equal cells. Top: azure skybird with golden beak and swept crest facing right; rounded moss-green forest sprite with ivory face and leaf ears; honey-brown wooden support plank; blue-gray stone block. Bottom: rustic Y-shaped slingshot frame with leather wrapping and no elastic; ivory impact puff; golden star; wildflower tuft. Hand-painted gouache sprites, shaded volumes, textured wood and feathers, readable silhouettes. No text, borders or background. Rendered original dimensions: 1536 × 1024.
