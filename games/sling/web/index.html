A NOTE FROM THE GARDEN

Good things take a little practice.

Twelve islands across four regions. Four flights per island, plus an extra flight in your satchel. Use a bloom bomb to clear a cluster or a star feather to pierce stone. Break wooden supports, nudge the sprites, and find your perfect arc. Stone takes two hits. Click a target to inspect it. Use ← / → to aim, ↑ / ↓ for power, and Space to launch. Escape cancels a pull. Every spare flight earns a bonus.
