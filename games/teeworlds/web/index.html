FIND YOUR FLOW

Jump. Hook. Outplay.

Move with A/D or the arrow keys. Space gives you a jump and one air jump. Aim with your mouse. Left click fires; right click throws your grappling hook.

Collect hearts, armor, and weapons around the map. Use keys 1–5 to switch weapons you have picked up. The highest score at the end of 60 seconds wins.

Practice has a simple training bot. Duel gives you a private room code for a friend. Touch controls are available; drag on the arena to aim.

Completed rounds are replayed through the original native engine, then recorded in a signed Myelin session. This release does not settle matches on CKB.
