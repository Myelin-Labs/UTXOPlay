About UTXOPlay

UTXOPlay is a small browser game store built around instant play and CKB-compatible wallet identity.

The launch collection

The Quiet Board is chess for two people sharing one device. Skyward is a single-player slingshot adventure across three floating islands. Teeworlds brings online duels and practice with a grappling hook and classic arena weapons. OpenStrike is a quick first-person duel in Neon Divide. All four are free to play.

Your game, remembered

Guest sessions are associated with a cookie in your browser. Wallet login opens a separate account library that you can access on another device. Guest progress is not automatically moved into a wallet account.

What runs on Myelin

Chess and Skyward rules run on our Rust servers; Teeworlds uses a native C++ engine; OpenStrike uses its original Rust simulation. Both record inputs for native replay checks. Myelin records signed execution histories, persists game state, and supports replay verification and evidence export. These games do not execute game transactions on CKB, settle wagers, or issue token rewards.

Art and music

Teeworlds uses original Teeworlds assets under CC BY-SA 3.0, with a new browser presentation. Teeworlds credits and source. Chess and Skyward game art was generated with OpenAI ImageGen for the Myelin Labs game projects. Skyward features “Carefree”; The Quiet Board features “Evening Fall (Harp)”, both by Kevin MacLeod under CC BY 4.0. Music is used unmodified. Full credits are available inside each game.

Interface credits

Store transitions use Motion under the MIT license. Icons use Lucide. Animation assets are served with the store, and motion can be reduced in Display preferences.

Developers

UTXOPlay Studio supports free and paid self-service publication. Vendors host their own Myelin instance, check its connection, then explicitly publish a saved draft. Free games have no listing charge. Paid games use one-time CKB mainnet purchases with a direct 20% Store / 80% vendor split. Read the hosting and publishing guide.
