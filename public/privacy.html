Privacy at UTXOPlay

UTXOPlay stores the information needed to authenticate you and save your games. This notice describes the Store and its vendor marketplace.

Accounts and cookies

We store your verified public CKB address, signing scheme, account identifier, game activity, and any developer projects you create. Login uses a signed challenge. We do not ask for or store your seed phrase or wallet private key. Challenge records expire after five minutes; login cookies expire after seven days.

Essential, HttpOnly cookies keep you signed in and bind login requests to your browser. Guest accounts use a separate random identifier. Clearing cookies can remove access to a guest save. Signing out revokes the current login session but does not delete saved games.

Community profiles and posts

Your chosen username, reviews, star ratings, recommendations and replies are public. We store these against your authenticated account, along with helpful votes, reports and moderation records. Public review responses do not include your wallet address or account identifier. Names may change; post ownership stays with the account. Reviews require a successful game action, not a measured playtime threshold.

You can edit your username and review or delete your visible reviews and replies. Deleting a review also deletes its replies and helpful votes. Reports and operator moderation records remain for abuse handling. Hidden posts require operator review. Do not include personal or wallet-sensitive information in public posts.

Game records

Our servers store game state, actions, and authority-signed Myelin records so games can recover and be replayed. Wallet accounts retrieve their own records after signing in. A game export is a copy you choose to download.

Purchases and vendor games

For purchases we store the buying account, game, price, vendor and Store receive addresses, transaction hash, prepared transaction, confirmation status and access grant. CKB transactions and their addresses are public on the blockchain. The buyer sees their receipts; the relevant vendor sees their sales and transaction links. The Store never receives a wallet private key or seed phrase and does not take custody of either revenue share.

Vendor games open on the vendor’s domain. A one-use ticket lets that vendor’s server receive a project-scoped player identifier and session expiry. We do not send your Store login cookie, wallet login proof or wallet address through the launch protocol. The vendor sees your IP address and browser requests, can set its own session cookie, and operates under its own policies. Public transaction links may reveal a buyer’s public address independently.

We store vendor drafts, published configuration, receive address, verification token and a hash of the vendor launch key. Publication makes the listing and game host public. Launch tickets expire after two minutes; the reference vendor session lasts at most 24 hours. Wallet-linked access grants have no scheduled expiry; continued gameplay depends on vendor hosting.

Third-party services

Connecting a wallet interacts with the selected wallet provider. CKB wallet software may query public network endpoints. JoyID login uses the JoyID credential API to verify that your signing credential belongs to your claimed account. These providers receive the information needed for that request and operate under their own policies.

Operational information

The hosting and HTTPS services may record IP addresses and request metadata for operation and abuse prevention. Application rate-limit counters expire automatically. UTXOPlay does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics in this release.

Display preferences

Your motion preference is saved locally in this browser. Store effects are bundled with the site; they do not send usage data to an animation provider.

Your choices

You can play as a guest, decline a wallet signature, sign out, or export your game. Saved accounts and games currently remain on the server; self-service account deletion is not available in this initial release.
